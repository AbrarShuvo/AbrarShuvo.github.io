The problem

New business arrived faster than anyone could file it.

Enquiries land in an inbox all week. Someone opens each one, copies the details into a spreadsheet and updates it later if it goes anywhere. The copying is dull, it comes in bursts, and in a busy week a lead quietly goes unanswered.

Before
Copying details in, email by email
The same lead entered twice
No quick view of the week
After
The tracker fills itself
Possible duplicates are flagged
One dashboard shows the whole week
How it works

Three steps

1

Collect

Reads new enquiry emails and pulls out who wrote, when, what they asked for and where they came from.

2

Clean

Tidies names and dates, spots possible duplicates and flags anything incomplete.

3

Publish

Writes everything into the Excel tracker the team already uses, and refreshes the dashboard.

InboxClean with pandasExcel trackerDashboard
Decisions I made

Built to be relied on

Excel, not a new app

The team already works in a spreadsheet. A new tool to learn would have been ignored.

Your edits always win

If someone changed a status or added a note by hand, the automation leaves it alone and only adds what’s new.

Nothing is ever deleted

A possible duplicate is flagged, not removed, so a wrong guess costs a glance, not a lost lead.

The result

The week’s leads, filed and visible, without anyone typing them in.
