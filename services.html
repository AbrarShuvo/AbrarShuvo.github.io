Questions people ask

How do I know it actually works?

Every project ships with automated tests, and you watch it run on your own data before final delivery. If it doesn't do what we agreed, you don't pay the rest.

Is my data safe?

I build with sample data wherever possible, and I ask you to remove private details before sending anything. Accounts and hosting stay in your name, not mine.

What if I'm in a different timezone?

I'm in Indonesia (UTC+7) and work with clients worldwide. Most of the work is async; I keep a window open in the evening for calls with Europe and the Americas.

What if my task is too small?

Then it's cheap and fast, which is a good place to start. Write to me anyway. If it isn't worth automating I'll tell you so rather than sell you something.

Not sure which one you need?

Describe the task in two sentences. I'll tell you what it would take, what it would cost, and whether it's worth doing at all.
