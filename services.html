What I can build for you

Four kinds of work. If your task doesn't fit neatly into one of them, send it anyway and I'll tell you honestly whether I can help.

Workflow automation

If someone on your team does the same thing by hand every week, it can probably run by itself.

Good for: Emails into a spreadsheet · weekly reports · sorting and renaming files · moving data between two tools

Typical time: 1–2 weeks

Ask about this
You get
  • A script that does the job end to end
  • Tests that show it works
  • Simple instructions to run it
  • Help if anything needs fixing after handover

AI integrations

AI where it saves real time: reading long text, pulling out the facts and sorting things into the right place. Every answer is checked against a fixed format, so you can rely on it.

Good for: Meeting notes into tasks · details pulled from documents · sorting leads, tickets or enquiries

Typical time: 2–3 weeks

Ask about this
You get
  • An AI step built into your workflow
  • Answers checked against a fixed format
  • Accuracy tested on real examples
  • A clear note on what it can’t do yet

Data dashboards

Your numbers are already in spreadsheets nobody opens. I clean them up and build one page that answers the question you ask every week.

Good for: Sales and lead tracking · weekly KPIs · data you can’t trust yet

Typical time: 1–2 weeks

Ask about this
You get
  • Your data cleaned and combined
  • A dashboard in Excel or on the web
  • An easy way to refresh it
  • A short walkthrough for your team

Websites

Business websites you can keep up to date yourself, with an admin panel for products and content. I also change and fix existing WordPress sites.

Good for: Company and catalog sites · quote and enquiry forms · WordPress changes and fixes

Typical time: 2–4 weeks

Ask about this
You get
  • Every page designed and approved before it’s built
  • An admin panel to update your content
  • Works on phones and desktops
  • Hosting accounts in your name

Questions

Things people ask

How do I know it actually works?

You see it running on your own data before anything is final, and every project comes with tests that check it does what we agreed.

Is my data safe?

I work with sample data wherever I can, and ask you to remove private details before sending files. Accounts and hosting stay in your name.

What if we’re in different time zones?

I’m in Indonesia (UTC+7) and work with clients worldwide. Most of the work happens in writing, and I keep evening hours open for calls with Europe and the Americas.

What do you need from me to start?

A real example of the task, a few sample files with private details removed, and one person who can answer questions.
