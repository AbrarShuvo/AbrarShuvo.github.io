The brief

Buyers need to trust you before they ask for a price.

Sourcing runs on trust: a buyer is paying for goods from overseas that they haven’t seen. The business needed a site that looks credible to buyers, and a way for the owner to keep products and services up to date without waiting on a developer.

What they needed
A catalog that looks credible to buyers
Quotes on request, with prices hidden by default
Full control of content, without code
What they got
Product categories and services, clearly laid out
A short quote form: product, quantity, destination
An admin panel for products, prices and services
How I built it

Three steps

1

Design every screen first

Every page, including the admin screens, was designed and approved in Figma before any code.

2

Keep version one small

Quotes only: no cart, checkout or customer accounts, so the site could launch sooner.

3

Hand over control

The owner signs in to update products, photos, prices and services. Enquiries arrive in one place.

Decisions I made

Built to be relied on

The client owns everything

Hosting and database accounts are in the client’s name, so nothing of theirs is locked up with me.

Prices hidden by default

Buyers ask for a quote, and the owner decides when prices should show.
